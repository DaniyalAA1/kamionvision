And what it will not say.

  • Measured tread depth, tire date codes, retread status, concealed dual sidewalls and opposite steer-tire condition
  • Brake linings, discs or drums and complete hub inspection
  • Air bags, shocks, steering and suspension condition
  • Fifth-wheel jaws, locking action and mounting play
  • Engine, driveline, fluid leakage and running condition
  • AdBlue tank, exhaust and DPF condition
  • Driver's seat, pedals and entry-step wear
  • Engine hours and running-engine warning-lamp check
